Our Loft Insulation Service In Loughor

Our loft insulation service in Loughor helps homeowners tackle heat loss through poorly insulated roof spaces, whether that means fitting new mineral wool insulation or bringing existing material up to a suitable depth.

Across Loughor, from established streets with mature trees and older properties to single-storey bungalows, loft spaces can vary considerably. Some have generous access and exposed joists, while others contain old insulation that has settled, been disturbed or compressed over the years.

At Warmline Swansea, we focus on getting the insulation right for the property. That means checking what is already there, considering ventilation and making sure any additional insulation is fitted without blocking the airflow needed at the eaves.

Common Loft Insulation Problems in Loughor Homes

Heat Escaping Through the Roof

If your upstairs rooms lose warmth quickly after the heating switches off, the loft is worth checking. In older Loughor homes, insulation may be thin, patchy or missing around difficult corners. Proper coverage helps reduce unnecessary heat loss and can lower the amount of heating needed.

Bungalows with Large Loft Areas

Bungalows often have a substantial ceiling area beneath the roof. When that space is poorly insulated, warmth can escape across a large surface. We pay particular attention to consistent coverage, including around loft hatches, pipework and the edges of the roof space.

Old Insulation That No Longer Performs Well

Not every loft needs the existing insulation ripped out. If the material is dry, clean and in reasonable condition, adding another layer may be the sensible option. Damp, damaged or contaminated insulation needs investigating before deciding whether removal is appropriate.
