llansamlet Professional Loft Insulation Fitting

Check the loft and agree the work

We inspect accessible areas for gaps, compressed insulation, signs of damp and ventilation restrictions. Access, storage boards and services are checked so the quote reflects the actual job.

Sound existing insulation may be suitable to retain. Wet or damaged material needs assessment, and any underlying moisture problem should be addressed before fresh insulation is laid.

Fit consistent coverage across the loft floor

Insulation is fitted between the ceiling joists, with a further layer across them where appropriate. This covers exposed timber and closes gaps that let warmth escape from the rooms below.

We select the depth to suit the material and loft. At the eaves, ventilation routes stay clear; around downlights and other fittings, the installation follows the relevant clearance requirements.

Finish around the details that matter

The loft hatch, water tank, pipework and any access route all need consideration. A better-insulated loft can become colder, so frost protection and safe access cannot be overlooked.

If you use the loft for storage, we explain how boarding can sit above the insulation without squashing it. Before finishing, we check coverage and show you what has been installed.

Keep more of the heat you pay for

Once fitted, suitable loft insulation starts reducing heat loss through the loft straight away. That can reduce heating demand and help lower bills, with savings depending on the existing insulation, heating system and how you use your home.

For cost-conscious households in Llansamlet, the aim is practical: rooms that hold warmth for longer, less heat wasted through the ceiling and a clear understanding of what the work costs.
